Tips and action items: 
- Look at Oxford University research and what they have done on theoretical modeling
- Big gap in high-fidelity modeling of single turbines and especially arrays/fences
- Better to have application-agnostic methodological development, as well as application-specific contributions. This helps to prevent the contributions of the project from being too tightly linked with one field/topic, which might fall out of favor with the US GOVT
- "methodologically interesting" > "novel topic"
- Useful to frame the project around the role of the US national labs: somewhere in between the riskiness/novelty of academic research and the conservativeness of industry
	- Ideally, solutions are simple insights for industry to adopt
